# CEO Workforce & Marketing Extension

The CEO snapshot now includes workforce and marketing blocks without requiring the Owner to open operational screens.

## Workforce
- active headcount
- present today
- late today
- worked hours MTD
- overtime minutes MTD
- sales per worked hour MTD

## Marketing
- active campaigns
- marketing spend MTD
- attributed transactions MTD
- attributed revenue MTD
- ROAS MTD

When no operational data exists, counts are zero and ratio metrics remain NULL rather than inventing performance.
